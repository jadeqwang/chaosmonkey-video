Animate the first frame exactly. We are inside an ink painting that has real depth. The camera travels very slowly and steadily sideways to the left, a short dolly of about a tenth of the picture's width, no zoom, no tilt. The five colossal finger-shaped stone pillars stand at different distances in a sea of cloud: the short one on the right is nearest, the tall middle one farthest, so as the camera travels they slide past one another with gentle parallax and the gaps between them open and close. Thin mist the same cream colour as the paper drifts between the pillars, veiling the far ones and the bases; the small scalloped cloud curls along the bottom roll slowly like a living sea, drawn by a brush as we watch. It stays fine ink line and pale ink wash on cream xuan paper with visible paper grain. Exactly five pillars, their tops always inside the frame. Nothing new appears: no figure, no sun, no birds, no mountains, no text, no colour change.
