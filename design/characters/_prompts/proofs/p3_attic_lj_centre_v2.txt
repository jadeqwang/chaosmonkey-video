A Chinese painting of night, ink and light colour on cream xuan paper, in one hand: fine-line baimiao outlines for the faces and hands with light colour washes, dark ink washes for the room, the paper grain showing through everywhere. The attic above a restaurant, 1988: sloped ceiling, floor mats, bare floorboards with thin bright amber lines of kitchen light between them; one pool of red neon light from a small dusty window (no readable letters). Faces drawn in delicate fine ink lines with a light flesh wash, as in a Song or Ming figure painting, not anime, not a photograph, not a digital render. No text, no signature, no seal, no border.

Close-up of the little girl's face and shoulders, dead centre of the frame, looking straight into the lens and holding the look a moment too long: deadpan, level, unblinking. Red neon light on one side of her face, the room behind her dark ink.

IMPORTANT: the little girl's hair is glossy jet-black ink (never brown, never blonde, never grey), even where the red light falls on it.

Little Jade, 1988, age 5: a small Chinese girl with a round face and full soft cheeks, straight glossy black hair with blunt bangs to the eyebrows and a small half-ponytail tied high at the back of the head, the rest hanging straight to her shoulders; small dark almond eyes, a tiny nose, a small closed mouth; the family's deadpan, watchful, unsmiling "resting oblivious face". She wears a child-size cropped white zip jacket with a stand collar, one orange band right across the chest and around both upper arms, and a small pale-blue dot on her left chest; under it loose dark pyjama-like trousers; bare feet. No headphones.
