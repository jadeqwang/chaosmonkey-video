A Chinese painting of night, ink and light colour on cream xuan paper, in one hand: fine-line baimiao outlines for the faces and hands with light colour washes, dark ink and dusk-violet washes for the room, the paper grain showing through everywhere. Faces drawn in delicate fine ink lines with a light flesh wash, as in a Song or Ming figure painting, not anime, not a photograph, not a digital render. No text, no signature, no seal, no border, no logos.

Seen from above and slightly to the side: the woman sits on top of a child's bed, back against the wall, legs stretched out under a pale blanket, the little girl asleep against her arm with her eyes closed and her cheek on the woman's sleeve; the open picture book lies on the blanket, and a small flashlight lying on the blanket still makes one round pool of white light on the pages. The woman looks down at the girl, a soft face. Everything outside the pool sinks into violet-grey ink.
