Chinese ink painting on unsized cream xuan paper in the manner of Liang Kai's splashed ink. In the lower-left corner, small, a steep rocky peak in a few axe-cut strokes; on its summit a long thin staff stands planted upright, and on top of the staff the monkey crouches, one hand raised flat above his eyes to shade them, looking out to the right over an unpainted sea. Everything else is bare paper: the sea and sky are the paper itself. Seen a little from below. The monkey is exactly the design in the reference images: lean splashed-ink body, small round black cap with exactly two very long pheasant plumes arcing back, a peach-shaped cinnabar field around the gold eyes, pale muzzle, a few ochre dabs for the tiger-skin kilt, the staff dark with a gold band at each end. Not the 1961 animation, no gold circlet, no headband, no armour. No border, no seal, no text, no signature. Flat even scan of the painting, no shadow, no vignette.
