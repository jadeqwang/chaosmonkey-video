An animation keyframe pose sheet: the same monkey performer painted four times in one row, full body, each figure filling most of the sheet's height.
CHARACTER
The cloud (figures 1 and 2): his somersault cloud (jindou yun) is a small, tight auspicious cloud drawn in the traditional way: a few curling scroll-ended ink outlines (ruyi cloud curls) with a pale ink-grey and white wash inside, flat on its base, about as long as he is tall, with a short trailing tail of curls; drawn in the same ink hand, not a photographic cloud, not cotton.
The four poses, left to right:
1. Riding the cloud: standing on the cloud, knees bent, leaning forward into the speed toward the viewer's left, the right hand flat above his eyes, the staff in the left hand held back, scarf ends streaming behind.
2. Tumbling on the cloud: in a tucked somersault, upside down, just above the cloud, which curls round under him.
3. Plucking a hair: standing, he has pulled a single hair from his left forearm and holds it up between finger and thumb close to his lips, cheeks puffed, about to blow on it; staff tucked under the left arm.
4. Seated: sitting cross-legged on the paper, upright, the left hand resting on the knee, the right hand raised open in front of him, palm up, a small calm commanding smile; the staff lying across his lap.
