An animation keyframe pose sheet: the same monkey performer painted four times in one row, full body, each figure filling most of the sheet's height, the phases of an acrobatic somersault (jindou) travelling to the viewer's left.
CHARACTER
The four phases, left to right:
1. Launch: springing up and forward to the viewer's left off both feet, arms swung up, the staff in the right hand, body stretched.
2. Tucked somersault in the air: rolled into a tight ball, upside down, knees to chest, gripping his shins, the staff held along his body in the right hand, scarf ends whipping round.
3. Layout somersault: stretched straight and upside down in the air, arms out, legs together.
4. Landing: dropping onto both feet in a low crouch, one hand touching the ground, the staff swung out behind him, about to rise.
