An animation keyframe pose sheet: the same monkey performer painted four times in one row, full body, head to boots, each figure filling most of the sheet's height, a staff-fighting form (Peking-opera martial dance with the staff).
CHARACTER
The four poses, left to right:
1. Staff flower: standing square to the viewer, knees a little bent, spinning the staff in front of his body, gripped in the middle by the right hand, the staff diagonal across his chest, the left hand open beside it ready to take it.
2. Staff flower overhead: the staff spinning flat above his head, held in the middle by the raised right hand, the left arm out to the side for balance, one foot lifted, scarf ends swinging.
3. Overhead strike: both hands on the staff raised high above his head and behind, body arched back, about to bring it down, weight on the back leg, the front foot reaching forward.
4. Low sweep: dropped into a deep crouch on the bent right leg with the left leg stretched out straight to the side, sweeping the staff low and level along the ground with both hands, looking along it.
