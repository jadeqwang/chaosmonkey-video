An animation keyframe pose sheet: the same monkey performer painted four times in one row, each figure filling most of the sheet's height, the four phases of one trick: he plants his long staff upright on the ground and climbs it to perch on its top. In every figure the staff stands perfectly vertical, its foot on the same ground level, and it is the same length in all four (as tall as the sheet allows, about twice his height): the staff stands on its own, held up only by him.
CHARACTER
The four phases, left to right:
1. Planting: standing beside the upright staff, both hands gripping it at chest and head height, one foot lifted onto the staff a little above the ground, looking up its length.
2. Climbing, low: halfway up the staff, gripping it with both hands above his head, knees drawn up and both feet clasped against the staff below him (the soles of the boots pressed to either side of the staff), his body hugging it, looking up.
3. Climbing, high: near the top, one hand on the top end of the staff, the other hand lower on it, one knee already up beside the top, pulling himself onto it.
4. Perched: crouched balanced on the very top end of the upright staff, both boots together on its tip, knees up, the left hand resting on the knee, the right hand flat above his eyes shading them as he looks far out to the viewer's left; scarf ends flying.
