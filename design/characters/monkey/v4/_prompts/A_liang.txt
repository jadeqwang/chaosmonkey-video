An animation keyframe pose sheet: the same monkey performer painted four times in one row, full body, head to boots, each figure filling most of the sheet's height.
CHARACTER
The four poses, left to right:
1. LIANGXIANG, the held opera pose of looking out into the distance: standing on his left leg, the right knee raised high in front of him with the boot pointed down, the right hand held flat above his eyes like a visor to shade them (palm down, fingers together, thumb visible), head turned to look far out to the viewer's left, the left hand holding the staff horizontally behind the small of his back, the scarf ends flying. Proud, sharp, perfectly balanced.
2. Standing upright and proud facing the viewer, feet together, in the formal martial salute (baoquan): the right hand a fist, the open left hand wrapped over the front of the fist, both held together in front of the chest at collar height, elbows out and level; the staff tucked vertically under the left arm, held against his side.
3. A deep lunge to the viewer's left, the front knee bent, the back leg straight, driving the staff out level at shoulder height with both hands, the front hand forward on the staff, the back hand at his hip; face looking along the staff, fierce.
4. Newborn: crouching low on the balls of his feet, knees wide, both hands on the ground in front of him like an animal, head up and alert, looking at the viewer with wide eyes, the staff lying beside him on the paper.
