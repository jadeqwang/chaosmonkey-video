## Rules (measured on the files written; see Verification)

- **The song is untouched.** `mix = song + layer`, sample-aligned, 24-bit; no normalising, compression, ducking or limiting
  of the song. Outside the cue windows the layer is exactly 0.0.
- **Under the song.** Each cue is levelled against the song's momentary loudness (BS.1770, 400 ms): a target median margin per
  cue (9-10 dB for the few loud-section accents, 14-20 dB for the quiet beds), never closer than 6 dB in any frame of the summed
  layer, and a peak cap per cue.
- **Never over a lyric.** Wherever a word sounds (vocal stem >= -40 LUFS in 50 ms windows and within 12 dB of its local peak)
  the whole layer sits >= 6 dB (fitted at 7) under the vocal stem. Beds duck on the layer only (smoothed gain curve); events
  take a lower fixed gain. Events were placed in the gaps between words wherever the picture allows; the ones that the brief
  ties to a sung word (the three 听话 notches, the 四击头 seals, the drop-2 seal echoes, the C1/J5 chop flips) are low-passed
  under the voice band and listed in the near-word table.
- **Never against the song's percussion.** Every event is snapped to a measured hit or beat (`hit:` within 40 ms, `beat:`
  within 60 ms) or placed in a measured gap; nothing runs on a constant tempo.
- **In key where tonal.** D minor pentatonic (D F G A C): the neon hum is a D2 series with the F#/E partials removed; the
  chisel ring was re-pitched from ~B7 to A7; the relay tick's ring is F6.
- **Silence is sacred.** The 75 ms hole at 15.440 and the 0.24 s near-silence 149.285-149.525 are forced to exact zero in the
  layer (the song's own residue there, < -50 dBFS, is left as it is: gating it would be editing the song).
- **No copyrighted recordings.** Every source is generated by us (prompts in `media/sound/sfx/gen/*.txt`, calls in
  `media/genlog.jsonl` and `docs/spend_ledger.jsonl`) or synthesised in numpy/scipy (recipes in `media/sound/sfx/*.txt`).
- **Beds respect the song's edits.** Every bed drops to zero inside each measured breakbeat chop and micro-silence after 1 s
  (6 ms ramps), so the song's stutters stay stutters. The car cabin also follows the song's own 125-250 Hz energy (held 3 dB
  under it) so it never fills the rap's bass drop-outs.

| `mix_v1_endC.wav` | **Click to black, then the room.** The song is cut at 237.840 (2 ms ramp under the relay tick, so the tick is the only click; the 0.48 s residue is dropped), then 3 s of faint attic room tone (-54 LUFS), which stops dead (3 ms). | 240.860 s |

**Recommendation: A.** The brief wants an abrupt, unresolved end: "one click, no fade-out, no reprise". In A, the song's own
hard cut does that: two switches (flashlight off on the last hit, neon off on the drone cut), and the music stops mid-air on a
non-zero sample. B is a fade-out by another name: the reverb tail resolves the drone, which is exactly what the brief rules
out, though it is the nicest sound of the three. C is honest but adds a third event (the room tone stopping) and 3 s of held
black that the picture would have to earn; keep it in reserve if the cut wants black before a title or credits. None of the
three softens the click itself.

- **The ink throw (58.673)** is held 27 dB under the song by the vocal rule ("formed" is held over the crash): it will be
  felt, barely heard. Raising it means breaking the rule for one cue, as HALYS did for "broke"; your call.
- **Words on the hits.** The 四击头 seals, the three 听话 notches, the drop-2 re-presses and the chop flips are tied by the
  treatment to moments where a word starts (within 0-110 ms). They are low-passed and sit >= 6.5 dB under the vocal, but they
  are in the near-word table below. Dropping the three small 才 seals and keeping only 仓 is the easy fallback.
- **Rap streetlights** cannot sit 150 ms clear of words (a syllable on every beat); they are soft swells with no transient.
- The near-silence 149.285-149.525 is exact zero in the layer, but the song itself is not digitally silent there (< -50
  dBFS residue). True digital silence would mean editing the song; not done.
- The mix's true peak is -3.1 dBTP (limit -1); integrated loudness moves +0.01 LU. Nothing was re-mastered.

**Method (by measurement, per cue; build_v2.py).**
1. *Spectral placement.* Each cue gets a static, power-preserving EQ (+-7 dB) that moves its energy into the 1/3-octave bands where the song is weakest in
   the cue's own window, discounts the bands laptop speakers and earbuds do not reproduce (< 150 Hz, > 6.3 kHz) and takes 3 dB out of 1-4 kHz (the speech region).
   Where it helped most: the neon ticks (-10 dB band margin from +15), the egg, the hair and the book (they moved into holes in the song's spectrum).
2. *Level by band margin.* Events: gain until the median band margin (song minus cue in the cue's own bands) reaches its target (6 dB; v1 had 9-25), limited by
   the cue alone staying >= 3 dB under the song's 400 ms loudness (v1: 6) and a -5 dBFS peak cap (v1: -8 to -30), never below v1, and a peak floor of -18 dBFS (before the vocal ride) so that
   nothing is left near the noise floor. Beds (neon hums, room tones, breath, wind, hair, whisper, car) are *ridden*: the bed follows the song in its own band at 2-8 dB under it,
   frame by frame (200 ms hold), so the room tones come up in the sparse sections (intro, band verse, build, end) and stay out of the song's holes.
3. *Vocal sidechain on the layer bus.* Where a word sounds in the vocal stem (`work/stems/vocals.wav`; 50 ms, >= -40 LUFS, within 12 dB of its local peak; +-20 ms dilated) the summed layer is
   reduced band by band (< 250, 250-4000, > 4000 Hz, K-weighted) to stay >= 6.5 dB under the vocal's same band, then >= 6 dB broadband, then a K-weighted polish on two interleaved
   frame grids. v1 lowered a whole event cue to its worst word; v2 rides frame by frame and acts on the summed layer, so overlapping cues share the headroom.
   Result on the final file: the layer is >= **6.1 dB** under the vocal stem in every word frame (rule 6; 5th percentile 8.5 dB).
4. *Peak guard.* A layer-only gain guard (never the song) keeps the mix under -1.6 dBFS sample peak: 29 ms of the layer touched, at most 0.72 dB. Clipped samples: 0.
